Welcome! I am currently a research associate in the Department of Comprehensive Dentistry at the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio. I previously studied Biomedical Engineering and Chemistry at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and North Carolina State University.

My academic pursuits are balanced between experimental and computational work for materials science. Despite the utility of polymers and their composites in many critical industries, our understanding of their processing techniques and their effects on bulk properties is still not entirely clear. At the bench, I study these materials functionalized with antimicrobials to reduce instances of secondary caries in direct restorations. Computationally, my work focuses on automating the extraction of legacy experimental data from literature to generate feature-rich datasets. This is in the hopes of aiding materials discovery within the broader scientific community to address pressing needs human health, energy, and broader industries.

Regarding my professional interests, my career objectives involve studying computational methods in order to complement experimentation for enabling autonomous, closed-loop systems of materials research and development.

Beyond academic and professional activities, I also enjoy cycling and ultimate frisbee.
